# FORMA exercise demonstration engineering review

Reviewed on 23 November 2026. The unit of review is `exerciseId` plus equipment. The findings below describe the original FORMA prototype and its correspondence to a reference. The `originalVolumeReviewPacket` function records the variant, revision, source provenance and deterministic SHA-256 across five poses. External references supplied no media, assets, or animation frames.

**Direct** means the source shows the movement and equipment. **Related** means at least one element of the setup, equipment or population differs. The passing checks cover source geometry and the Android presentation. Every variant remains `SOURCE_ONLY` until the qualified technique review specified by the product requirements.

| Exercise ID | Reference and scope | Finding on the FORMA prototype |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `squat` | [NHS strength exercises][nhs], related mini-squat | Known direction through hip and knee flexion. A fixed toe or heel contact through the whole cycle and individual depth remain unverified. |
| `wall_push` | [South Tees NHS press-up progression][push], direct wall variant | Fixed hand plane and foot position. The prototype does not establish alignment at all intermediate phases. |
| `bird_dog` | [ACE Bird-dog][bird], direct | Opposite arm and leg reach, then both hands and knees return to support before the side switch. |
| `prone_w` | [ACE prone shoulder series][prone], direct W formation | Pelvis remains low and fixed. Forearms point toward the head and move up from the prone setup. Scapular motion remains schematic. |
| `calf` | [North Tees NHS standing exercises][heel], direct | Added a fixed hand support and toe contact while the heels rise. |
| `goblet` | [NASM goblet squat coaching][goblet-guide], direct dumbbell option | Both grips converge on one chest-held dumbbell. Foot contact and depth remain unverified. |
| `db_row` | [Mayo Clinic bent-over dumbbell row][db-row], related supported single-arm variant | FORMA draws two loads and the elbows move back. A direct bilateral setup and segment lengths need review. |
| `floor_press` | [Dumbbell Floor Press exercise guide][floor], direct movement, secondary source | Both loads rise from the near-floor upper-arm position. Contact and wrist/forearm alignment remain provisional. |
| `raise` | [NASM lateral raise demonstration][raise], direct | Bilateral raising in depth. Individual shoulder limits, wrist orientation and load are outside the rig. |
| `curl` | [Mayo Clinic dumbbell curl][curl], related unilateral variant | Demonstrates forearm flexion with two loads. Hand supination and stable elbow orientation remain schematic. |
| `bb_row` | [CSCCa barbell bent-over row][bb-row], direct | One bar joins both grips and moves toward the torso. Verify precise trunk stability and bar/torso clearance. |
| `chair_squat` | [South Tees NHS sit-to-stand][chair], related sitting end position | Stationary seat and lowered hip contact. FORMA uses a touch-and-rise variant. |
| `bridge` | [North Tees NHS lower-limb exercises][bridge], direct movement, physiotherapy context | Hip rises with the shoulder and ankles fixed. The floor contact and top alignment are schematic. |
| `incline_push` | [NASM incline push-up][incline], direct | Hands meet a fixed elevated support. Verify a straight body line during the lowering phase. |
| `dead_bug` | [NASM dead bug][dead], direct | Opposite reach and return to the neutral setup before switching. Individual lumbar control cannot be measured from the mesh. |
| `db_rdl` | [NASM dumbbell Romanian deadlift][db-rdl], direct | Fixed feet and a visible hip hinge. Load clearance from the legs throughout the motion still needs review. |
| `hammer` | [NASM dumbbell hammer curl demonstration][hammer], direct | Elbow flexion is visible. The simplified hand mesh cannot demonstrate the neutral grip. |
| `db_press` | [NASM dumbbell overhead press demonstration][db-press], related overhead-press movement | Upward dumbbell travel. FORMA depicts the standing variant, with standing stability and forearm alignment still under review. |
| `bb_rdl` | [NASM barbell Romanian deadlift][bb-rdl], direct | Separate barbell load and a visible hinge. The bar-to-leg clearance and unchanging segment lengths are unverified. |
| `bb_curl` | [NASM barbell biceps curl][bb-curl], direct | One bar connects the two grips and moves via elbow flexion. Wrist orientation is schematic. |
| `wall_slide` | [South Tees NHS lateral wall slides][slide], related wall orientation | FORMA depicts back to the wall and keeps both arms on a posterior plane. The variant and its shoulder limits need an exact reference. |
| `self_curl` | [Self-resisted curl exercise guide][self], direct movement, secondary source | The assisting wrist now follows the working forearm. The prototype cannot establish the direction or amount of applied resistance. |
| `band_row` | [ACE standing tubing row][band-row], related partner anchor | The rig uses a fixed forward anchor and a pull toward the torso. The source uses a partner; anchorage safety requires its own check. |
| `band_press` | [NSCA resistance movement progressions][nsca], related | The line of resistance runs to a fixed anchor behind the figure. Verify the exact standing setup and attachment. |
| `band_extension` | [ACE triceps pressdown][pushdown], related cable version | Bands now run toward a fixed high point in front, consistent with downward elbow extension. Validate the exact band attachment and resistance control. |
| `kettlebell_squat` | [NASM goblet squat][goblet], direct kettlebell version | Two hands now share the same load and its handle. Verify feet and depth throughout the movement. |
| `kettlebell_deadlift` | [ACE deadlift coaching][elevated] and [NASM kettlebell deadlift][kb], related floor setup | Added a stationary elevation contacted by the bell at rest. Check the exact elevated variant and height independently. |
| `scap_pull` | [NASM pull-up][pullup], related overhead grip and shoulder setup | Hands stay on the bar and feet on a fixed support while the upper body rises slightly. No direct confirmation of this assisted variant. |
| `mat_plank` | [NASM plank][plank], direct | Isometric hold. Forearm and toe supports remain fixed. The interface offers orbit and text instructions for the hold. |
| `bench_step` | [Mayo Clinic step-up][step], direct low platform movement | Leading foot planted throughout, trailing foot clears the edge before crossing it. Return and side change are described in the text. |
| `cable_triceps` | [ACE triceps pressdown][pushdown], direct | Both hands share a handle that moves below a fixed pulley. Elbow path remains schematic. |
| `lat_pulldown` | [ACE seated lat pulldown][lat], direct | Fixed high pulley, seated support and thigh restraint with a moving transverse grip. Shoulder and elbow alignment still need a qualified pass. |
| `leg_press` | [NASM leg press][leg-press] and [ACE seated leg press][seated-leg-press], related machine configurations | Fixed seat and guide rails, moving footplate, equal segment lengths throughout the knee path. Machine geometry and range still need review. |
| `chest_press` | [NASM chest press machine][chest], direct movement, machine configuration varies | Fixed seat and separate guided handles at chest level. This is a stylized guided model of the equipment. |
| `machine_row` | [NASM close-grip seated machine row][machine-row], related machine configuration | Fixed low pulley and seat, moving transverse grip. Verify equipment-specific supports and handle design. |
| `leg_curl` | [NASM seated leg curl][leg-curl], direct seated variant | Fixed knee axis and seat, constant shin radius and moving roller. Verify setup and exact roller placement. |
| `smith_squat` | [Core Health & Fitness vertical Smith product sheet][smith], related equipment reference | Fixed vertical bar guide. The prototype does not show the safeties or complete rack/unrack sequence. |

The on-screen walkthrough retains text for setup, movement, return and self-check. Source regressions verify representative contacts and fixed guides across multiple phases. GPU and accessibility evidence is tracked in Issue #9 and the product PR. Source hashes identify geometry revisions. Reviewer sign-off and physical acceptance have separate evidence requirements.
