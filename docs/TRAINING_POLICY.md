# FORMA training policy and sources
Reviewed 2026-10-14. Applies to local suggestions for adults, not clinical diagnosis or an individual medical prescription.

Sources reviewed:
- ACSM 2026 position stand overview: https://acsm.org/science-spotlight-acsm-releases-new-position-stand-on-resistance-training/
- ACSM 2026 guidelines summary: https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/
- NHS strength and flexibility: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/how-to-improve-strength-flexibility/
- NHS home strength exercises: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-exercises/

Implementation choices (conservative product defaults, not values individually validated by these sources): at most 3 separated strength days; 2 sets on first use/return after 21 days and at most 3 otherwise; 4 basic muscles plus up to 2 priorities; 8/12/15 reps; 5% suggested increase only after complete results, low effort, no pain and adequate recovery; return suggestion 70% previous weight. A latest stopped/pain session blocks increase. The user can lower or manually choose weight each set. No 1RM testing, training-to-failure requirement, automated medical eligibility or diagnosis.

20 original text-only exercise instructions and common mistakes. No third-party GIF/photos/fonts copied. Bodyweight chest and back remain available without racks/benches. Barbell-only users are not assigned exercises requiring absent safety racks. Equipment-compatible replacement selection uses the same muscle and resets proposed weight to zero.

Limitations or severe poor recovery block automatic generation. Pain/discomfort stops the workout, stores the gate and never records an invented completed set. OEM audio and clinical suitability remain physical/user review boundaries.
